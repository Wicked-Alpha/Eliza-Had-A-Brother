# Sources

Everything this archive states comes from one of these. Primary sources are documents from the time or from the people involved. Secondary sources are later accounts.

## The 1972 encounter

- V. Cerf, "PARRY Encounters the DOCTOR," RFC 439, NIC 13771, 21 January 1973. Primary. [RFC Editor](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc439.html). Full text in [Exhibit 01](exhibits/01-rfc-439/).
- R. Kahn, "Demonstration at International Computer Communications Conference," RFC 371, NIC 11020, 12 July 1972. Primary. [RFC Editor](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc371.html). Full text in [Exhibit 02](exhibits/02-rfc-371/).
- *Scenarios for using the ARPANET at the International Conference on Computer Communication*, NIC 11863, October 1972. Primary. [Computer History Museum PDF](https://archive.computerhistory.org/resources/access/text/2019/07/102784024-05-001-acc.pdf).
- RFC 597, Host Status, December 1973. Primary. [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc597).
- Vint Cerf, oral history, Charles Babbage Institute, OH 191. Primary. [University of Minnesota](https://conservancy.umn.edu/bitstream/handle/11299/107214/oh191vgc.pdf).
- ACM A.M. Turing Award biography of Vint Cerf. Secondary. [ACM](https://amturing.acm.org/award_winners/cerf_1083211.cfm).

## ELIZA

- *Computer conversations, 1965*, Joseph Weizenbaum personal archives, MIT, MC-0383. Primary. [MIT Dome item](https://dome.mit.edu/handle/1721.3/201699); [collection page](https://dome.mit.edu/handle/1721.3/201698).
- R. Lane, A. Hay, A. Schwarz, D. M. Berry and J. Shrager, "ELIZA Reanimated." Secondary, by the team that restored the code. [arXiv 2501.06707](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.06707v1).
- J. Shrager, "ELIZA Reinterpreted." Secondary. [arXiv 2406.17650](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.17650v2).
- ELIZAGEN, Original ELIZA. [Project page](https://sites.google.com/view/elizagen-org/original-eliza).
- rupertl/eliza-ctss. [GitHub](https://github.com/rupertl/eliza-ctss).
- Deutsche Biographie, Joseph Weizenbaum. Secondary. [Entry](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118820591.html).
- Computer History Museum catalogue, *Computer Power and Human Reason* (1976). [Record](https://www.computerhistory.org/collections/catalog/102635737).

## PARRY

- K. M. Colby, S. Weber and F. D. Hilf, "Artificial Paranoia," *Artificial Intelligence* 2(1), 1971, pages 1 to 25. Primary. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702%2871%2990002-6); [scanned copy](https://courses.cs.umbc.edu/471/papers/colby_71.pdf).
- K. M. Colby, F. D. Hilf, S. Weber and H. C. Kraemer, "Turing-like indistinguishability tests for the validation of a computer simulation of paranoid processes," *Artificial Intelligence* 3, 1972, pages 199 to 221. Primary. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702%2872%2990049-5).
- K. M. Colby, files from the Stanford AI Lab: draft preface, dated 11 December 1973 ([PREF](https://saildart.org/PREF[4,KMC]13)); chapter draft, dated 18 February 1974 ([CHAP6](https://saildart.org/CHAP6[4,KMC])); and, with F. D. Hilf, "How to Use and How Not to Use Turing-like Tests in Evaluating the Adequacy of Simulation Models," dated 28 February 1973 ([TTT](https://saildart.org/TTT[4,KMC])). Primary.
- K. M. Colby, *Artificial Paranoia: A Computer Simulation of Paranoid Processes*, Pergamon, 1975. [Wellcome Collection record](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/pvxkk7yg).
- PARRY source, CMU Artificial Intelligence Repository. [Listing](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/Groups/AI/util/areas/classics/parry/0.html); [readme](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/Groups/AI/util/areas/classics/parry/readme.txt).
- University of California, In Memoriam, Kenneth Mark Colby. Secondary. [Entry](https://senate.universityofcalifornia.edu/_files/inmemoriam/html/KennethMarkColby.htm).

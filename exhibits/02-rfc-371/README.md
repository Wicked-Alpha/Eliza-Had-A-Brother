## Why it is here

This is the document that fixes the date and place of the conference later retellings attach the PARRY and DOCTOR conversation to.

Kahn announces a network demonstration to run alongside the conference, scheduled for 24 to 26 October 1972 at the Washington, D.C. Hilton Hotel. The plan is to deliver a TIP to the Hilton, connect it to the ARPANET, and wire every terminal at the hotel to it, so that attendees can sit down and use resources on the network themselves.

The conversation in RFC 439 is dated 18 September 1972, 36 days before this conference opened. See [Exhibit 07](../07-iccc-1972/) for what was shown there.

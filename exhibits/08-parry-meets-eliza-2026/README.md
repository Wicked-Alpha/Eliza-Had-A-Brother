# Exhibit 08: PARRY meets ELIZA, 2026

In 1972, PARRY talked to a DOCTOR descended from Bernie Cosell's Lisp version ([Exhibit 03](../03-cosell-doctor/)). Weizenbaum's own original ELIZA, written in MAD-SLIP at MIT, was missing until 2021 and did not run again until December 2024 ([Exhibit 04](../04-eliza-1965/)).

## What happened

On 26 April 2026, the ELIZAGEN project published "PARRY Parries Again." It reports that Lars Brinkhoff revived PARRY on a 1974 WAITS system image, and that Rupert Lane then copied turns by hand between PARRY and Weizenbaum's original ELIZA.

That is the earliest exchange between PARRY and Weizenbaum's own ELIZA that this archive has found, more than half a century after the session in RFC 439.

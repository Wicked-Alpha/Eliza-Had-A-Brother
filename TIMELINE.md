# Timeline

Every date below comes from the exhibit or source linked beside it.

| Date | Event | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1963 | Weizenbaum's paper on SLIP, the list-processing package later used to write ELIZA, thanks Kenneth Colby for his advice and support. | [ELIZA Reinterpreted, arXiv 2406.17650](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.17650v2) |
| 1965 | MIT dates the surviving ELIZA listing, *Computer conversations*, to this year. | [Exhibit 04](exhibits/04-eliza-1965/) |
| September 1965 | Weizenbaum's ELIZA paper is received by *Communications of the ACM*. | [Exhibit 04](exhibits/04-eliza-1965/) |
| January 1966 | The ELIZA paper is published, CACM vol. 9, no. 1, pages 36 to 45. | [Exhibit 04](exhibits/04-eliza-1965/) |
| 1966 | Colby, Watt and Gilbert publish a Stanford therapy-dialogue program, crediting Weizenbaum of MIT with the first program of the type. | [Journal of Nervous and Mental Disease 142(2), Stanford scan](https://stacks.stanford.edu/file/druid:hk334rq4790/hk334rq4790.pdf) |
| 2 July 1966 | Date on the script tape of a PDP-1 BBN-LISP DOCTOR, labelled SCRIP 4.1, which ELIZAGEN files under Cosell's name. | [Exhibit 03](exhibits/03-cosell-doctor/) |
| 4 January 1971 | "Artificial Paranoia" by Colby, Weber and Hilf is accepted by *Artificial Intelligence*. The model it describes was later called PARRY. | [Exhibit 05](exhibits/05-parry-1971/) |
| 1972 | Colby, Hilf, Weber and Kraemer publish the indistinguishability tests, *Artificial Intelligence* vol. 3. | [Exhibit 06](exhibits/06-psychiatrist-tests/) |
| 13 June 1972 | Date on the page headers of Cosell's 1972 DOCTOR listing. | [Exhibit 03](exhibits/03-cosell-doctor/) |
| 12 July 1972 | Bob Kahn announces the ARPANET demonstration for ICCC in RFC 371. | [Exhibit 02](exhibits/02-rfc-371/) |
| 18 September 1972 | PARRY, at SAIL, and the DOCTOR, at BBN-TENEX, hold a session reached from UCLA. | [Exhibit 01](exhibits/01-rfc-439/) |
| 24 to 26 October 1972 | ICCC at the Washington Hilton. PARRY and the DOCTOR are listed as separate hands-on demos. | [Exhibit 07](exhibits/07-iccc-1972/) |
| 21 January 1973 | Vint Cerf publishes the transcript as RFC 439, "PARRY Encounters the DOCTOR." | [Exhibit 01](exhibits/01-rfc-439/) |
| December 1973 | Colby's draft preface credits Sylvia Weber Russell with the original version of the program. | [Exhibit 05](exhibits/05-parry-1971/) |
| July 1974 | Weizenbaum's letter "Automating psychotherapy" in CACM parodies the PARRY validation. | [Exhibit 06](exhibits/06-psychiatrist-tests/) |
| 1975 | Colby publishes *Artificial Paranoia: A Computer Simulation of Paranoid Processes*. | [Exhibit 05](exhibits/05-parry-1971/) |
| 1976 | Weizenbaum publishes *Computer Power and Human Reason*. | [Exhibit 04](exhibits/04-eliza-1965/) |
| 5 September 1991 | Martin Frost writes the readme for PARRY's source in the CMU AI Repository. | [Exhibit 05](exhibits/05-parry-1971/) |
| December 1999 | RFC 439 is put into machine-readable form for the online RFC archives. | [Exhibit 01](exhibits/01-rfc-439/) |
| 20 April 2001 | Kenneth Colby dies. | [Exhibit 05](exhibits/05-parry-1971/) |
| 5 March 2008 | Joseph Weizenbaum dies. | [Exhibit 04](exhibits/04-eliza-1965/) |
| 2021 | The original ELIZA listing is found in Weizenbaum's papers at MIT. | [Exhibit 04](exhibits/04-eliza-1965/) |
| 12 May 2023 | MIT puts the digitized listing online. | [Exhibit 04](exhibits/04-eliza-1965/) |
| 21 December 2024 | The original ELIZA runs again on an emulated IBM 7094. | [Exhibit 04](exhibits/04-eliza-1965/) |
| 26 April 2026 | ELIZAGEN reports PARRY revived and relayed by hand to Weizenbaum's original ELIZA. | [Exhibit 08](exhibits/08-parry-meets-eliza-2026/) |
